I'm Gio, an Italian neuroscientist based in Paris. I'm studying Cognitive and Behavioural Neuroscience at Sorbonne Université, supervised by Mehdi Khamassi.

## Research

My work sits between two fields: EEG and brain–computer interfaces on one side, and reinforcement learning models of behaviour on the other. I'm interested in how people handle uncertainty, and in what drives curiosity and exploration.
